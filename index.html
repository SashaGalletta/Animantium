The person behind the project

A little about me

A student-made resource to make Biology clearer, more visual, and easier to learn.

Hi, I'm Narella Galletta.

I'm a student preparing for university. I created Animantium to help other students understand Biology more easily, one topic at a time.

My journey

At the age of twelve, I moved to Spain, where I began studying the IGCSE curriculum. Biology became my favourite subject, inspiring me to create this website. I hope Animantium will make learning Biology easier for future students by explaining each topic clearly and visually.

Explanations

I explain each topic in a simple but detailed way, so everyone can understand it.

Visuals

I create my own illustrations to make Biology more visual, engaging, and easier to understand.

Syllabus

I'll follow the Cambridge IGCSE Biology 0610 syllabus to cover all the required topics.
